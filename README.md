# Vietnam - IFRS Statements from the VAS Books

Odoo 19 module by Tricasia. The statutory books stay under VAS; IFRS statements are the VAS figures plus the entries of journals marked *IFRS Adjustments Only*.

- Journal setting `Ledger`: *VAS and IFRS* (default), *VAS Only*, *IFRS Adjustments Only*.
- IFRS statements (IAS 1, IAS 7): financial position, profit or loss and OCI, cash flows, consolidated financial position and profit or loss. Mapped from the leaf lines of B01-DN so every account is covered.
- Consolidation under IFRS: goodwill is not amortised, and net assets at acquisition include the IFRS adjustments booked before the acquisition (IFRS 3).
- An *IFRS Adjustments* journal is created for every company on the Vietnamese chart at installation.

Report definitions are generated by `tools/vn_accounting/gen_reports.py` in [Tricasia/odoo-dev](https://github.com/Tricasia/odoo-dev). Documentation: `docs/vn_accounting/VN_Accounting_Documentation.pdf`, section 15.

Tests: `odoo -d <tmp_db> -i l10n_vn_ifrs --test-tags /l10n_vn_ifrs --stop-after-init`
